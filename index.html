Import from CSV

Choose a CSV exported from Goodreads (My Books → Import/Export → Export Library) or from The Catalogue.

  • Books already in your catalogue (matched by title + author) won't be duplicated — if the file has genres they're missing, those get added in.
  • Goodreads shelves map to Want to read / Reading / Read.
  • Your non-standard bookshelves (anything besides to-read/currently-reading/read) come in as genres, comma-separated.
  • Files exported from The Catalogue keep their genre, year, status, rating and notes.
